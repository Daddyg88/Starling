The default STARLING voice pack.

  pack.json       name and details
  dialogue.json   every line she can say (edit with Notepad)
  clone/          the recording her voice is copied from (Tetko_Issued_Notice.mp3)

All lines are text. They are spoken by her cloned voice (Kokoro), made from the
recording in the clone folder. Edit any line in dialogue.json and she will say the new
words. Run run_build_voice_cache.bat afterwards so the new words play instantly.
