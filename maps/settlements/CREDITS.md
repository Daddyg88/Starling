# Settlement map credits

The Odyssey settlement maps (where the terminals, locked doors, power regulator and alarm console are) are based on the
**Odyssey Map Guide (OMG)** by **CMDR Quizengine** (https://elitedangereuse.fr/outils/quizengine/omg_1.1.html).

CMDR Quizengine has passed away. We are told it was his wish for the guide to be used and shared. Thank you, Commander.
The guide also thanks the fellow commanders who shared settlements with him.

Starling's maps are simple wireframe redrawings (building outlines and marker positions), not copies of his pictures.
Positions are traced by hand, so they can be a little off: if you spot a mistake, tell us.
