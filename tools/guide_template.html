STARLING

Fly further  •  Know more  •  Never alone

Starling is a voice companion for Elite Dangerous. She listens, presses your own keys, plots routes and talks back. This guide takes you from the download to your first jump. Do the five setup steps in order. They take about an hour, and nearly all of that is her getting ready while you wait.

Before you fly

Setup, in order

Each step needs the one before it. In particular, pick her voice before she learns her lines.

  1. 1

    Get Starling running

    Download Starling.exe from the newest release on the Starling GitHub page and double-click it. Keep your internet on. The first start installs everything she needs and takes several minutes. It is not frozen.

    • Windows blue box? Press More info, then Run anyway.
    • Antivirus removed it? Restore it from quarantine and allow it.
    • After that she updates herself each time you open her.
  2. 2

    Work down the Get started page

    Starling opens on a checklist. Anything that is not ticked has a button.

    • Elite Dangerous: start the game once and reach the main menu so she can find your journal.
    • Listening to you: press Turn listening on, then close and reopen Starling once.
    • How well she hears you: press Use Balanced if it is offered. She understands you far better.
  3. 3

    Choose her voice

    Open the Voice page and pick the voice you want to live with. Press Say hello to hear it. There is a cloned voice, plus built-in female and male voices.

    Choose once

    Every voice has its own set of recorded lines. If you change voice later, she has to learn all her lines again. Settle on one now.

  4. 4

    Let her learn her lines

    She has a great many sentences to say. She makes each one once, saves it, and from then on it plays instantly. Press Prepare her voice (on Get started or the Voice page) and let her work.

    The first time: leave her for about an hour

    • Do not play Elite while she does this. It uses a lot of your processor, and the game would stutter.
    • A progress bar shows how far she is. You can close Starling and carry on later. Lines she has already made are kept.
    • If you fly before she is done, she still talks, but some lines start 3 to 10 seconds late while she makes them on the spot.
    • This is a one-off. Updates add only a few new lines, so after an update press Prepare her voice once more and it is quick. She also does it quietly when you are docked or in a menu.
  5. 5

    Set your keys in Elite

    She presses your keyboard keys when you give a command, so Elite needs a key for each thing she does. This is the step people skip. Read the next section, then press Check my Elite keys on Get started.

The one thing in the game

Your Elite keys

Open Elite, then Options > Controls. Give each action below a keyboard key, press APPLY, then check again in Starling. She never changes your controls.

  • UI Panel Select must be a key you cannot type with. Use Enter. Select confirms nearly everything: docking, launching, the Nomad and plotting routes. When she plots a route she pastes the system name into the galaxy map search bar and presses Select while that bar is open. If Select is a typing key (a letter, a number or the space bar), she types that character into the search bar and the system name is ruined. Put Select on Enter, or another key that does not type, such as a numpad key, and make sure nothing else uses it. It needs to be on the keyboard, because she cannot press joystick buttons.
  • Keyboard keys onlyShe can only press keys. A joystick-only button cannot be pressed by voice, so give the action a keyboard key as well. Your joystick keeps working.
  • No shared keysIf two actions share a key, Elite may do the wrong thing. Testers found a shared panel key dropping out of the menu halfway through a command. Her key check lists any clashes.
  • Keep the menu keys off typing and flying keysUI Panel Up, Down, Left, Right and Back should be keys that neither type nor fly. The arrow keys are a good start, and a numpad key suits Back. Avoid letters, numbers, the space bar and Backspace (they edit the search bar), and avoid W, A, S and D (they fly the ship).

Using her

Talking to her

Say her name, then what you want. Speak clearly and a little slower than normal.

  • Wake wordThe default is "Starling". You can change it on the Listening page, for example to "Computer". Say it first, every time: Starling, landing gear down.
  • If she mishears youPick your microphone on the Listening page, make sure listening quality is Balanced, then teach her your voice.
  • Teach her your voiceSay Starling, teach me your voice or press Teach her my voice on Get started. She asks for about two dozen short phrases, one at a time (no wake word needed), and learns how you sound. It takes about three minutes and is a big help with a strong accent.
  • Hearing reportSay Starling, hearing report. She saves what she heard against what was meant to a text file you can send to whoever looks after her.

Twitch and YouTube chat

For streamers

Your viewers can talk to Starling from your Twitch chat, your YouTube live chat, or both at once. She answers their questions out loud on your stream, and they can flip a few harmless things on your ship. It only reads chat: Twitch needs no password or login, YouTube needs a free key (step 2), and she only ever presses your own Elite keys.

  • 1. Switch it onOpen the Streaming page, type your Twitch channel name, tick Let chat talk to Starling and press Save settings. The status line turns green when she is listening.
  • 2. YouTube (one-time key)Go to console.cloud.google.com, make a project, open APIs & Services, enable YouTube Data API v3, then Credentials → Create credentials → API key. Paste the key into Your YouTube API key. Each time you go live, paste your stream link into Your YouTube live stream link and press Save settings. Keep the key private. YouTube gives each key a free daily allowance that lasts about 5 hours of chat; when it runs out she says so and tries again later. She ignores anything said before she connected.
  • 3. Try it firstUse the test box on that page. Type !lights or !starling what is a neutron star? and press Send as a viewer. It goes through the same rules as real chat.
  • 4. Viewers type!starling and a question, or one of the ship words: !lights, !nightvision, and (if you allow them) !headlook, !scoop, !gear.
  • You are in chargeChoose who may use it (everyone, subscribers, moderators), switch each ship action on or off, set cooldowns, and block words. Say "pause the chat" any time to stop it, and "chat on" to bring it back.
  • Kept safeOnly the fixed list of words works. Landing gear is off by default, and even when it is on she refuses it while docked, landed, in supercruise or near anything big. Actions also wait while a menu is open or you are not in your ship. Viewers can never make her learn things about you: chat questions use a separate, read-only brain.
If something goes wrong

Help

  • She starts talking lateHer lines are not prepared yet. Press Prepare her voice and give her time.
  • A voice command does nothingPress Check my Elite keys. Most of the time the action has no keyboard key or shares one.
  • Send a logOpen the Developer page, press Copy my log to the clipboard and paste it into a message to whoever looks after Starling for you.
